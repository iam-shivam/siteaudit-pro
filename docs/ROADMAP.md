# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Safe public MVP

- URL/profile input and passive scan
- Adaptive rule engine
- Evidence-first report, health, and coverage
- Filters and JSON export
- Responsive, accessible UI
- Private deployment and real-target safety validation

**Done when:** a permitted public site produces deterministic findings and unsafe destinations are rejected.

## Phase 2 — Multi-page reports

- Bounded sitemap/representative-page discovery
- Screenshots and PDF export
- Report branding and share links
- De-duplication across pages
- Scan history and before/after comparison

## Phase 3 — Verified-owner workspace

- Domain verification
- Organizations, roles, and saved projects
- Owner questionnaire and evidence uploads
- Read-only analytics/repository/configuration integrations
- Disputes, exceptions, and retests

## Phase 4 — Launch readiness

- Staging and test-account workflows
- UX state/flow test plans
- Dependency, configuration, CI/CD, backup, and monitoring evidence
- Written authorization and immutable scope records
- Isolated authorized testing workers

## Phase 5 — Continuous assurance

- Scheduled rescans and material-change alerts
- Evidence freshness/expiry
- Policy and document drift
- Agency white-labeling, CRM integration, and remediation tracking

Active security testing remains a distinct service and permission boundary, never an automatic expansion of public scanning.
