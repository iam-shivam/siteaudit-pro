# Product and website document matrix

This matrix drives recommendations, not automatic legal conclusions. Applicability depends on countries served, customer type, data collected, contracts, regulated activity, and local law. A public scan can check presence and obvious consistency; qualified counsel determines legal adequacy.

| Site/product | Common public pages | Internal operational evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Brochure/local service | Privacy when data is collected, contact/business identity, cookie choices where applicable, website terms, accessibility information, booking/cancellation terms | Data/vendor inventory, retention schedule, content/accessibility review, incident contacts, backup procedure |
| Ecommerce | Privacy, cookies, store terms, shipping/delivery, returns/refunds, cancellation, warranty, payment/tax information, complaints/contact | Refund/fulfillment SOP, fraud/chargeback process, payment-provider scope, retention/deletion, incident response, tested restores |
| SaaS/subscription | Privacy, terms, acceptable use, billing/renewal/cancellation, account deletion/export, cookie choices, security/trust, support/status, DPA and subprocessors for B2B | Threat model, access matrix, secure release process, vulnerability management, monitoring, incident response, restore tests, business continuity |
| AI product | Applicable SaaS pages plus AI disclosure, limitations/human review, input/output and training-use terms, ownership, prohibited use, safety escalation | Model/provider inventory, data provenance, AI risk assessment, prompt/tool controls, evaluations, human oversight, AI incident/rollback plan |
| Marketplace/UGC | Buyer/seller terms, fees/payouts, refunds/disputes, prohibited content/items, moderation/reporting/appeals, IP complaints | Seller verification where applicable, fraud/abuse playbooks, moderation SOP, payout reconciliation, retention, incident response |
| Content/affiliate | Privacy, cookies, affiliate/sponsorship disclosure, copyright/licensing, editorial/corrections, community rules | Editorial approvals, fact checking, rights register, ad/tracker register, moderation, backup/security process |

## Feature overrides

- **Accounts:** reset/recovery, account deletion/export, support, and acceptable-use requirements.
- **Payments/subscriptions:** billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and receipts.
- **Uploads or user content:** content rules, moderation, reporting, appeals, retention, and deletion.
- **Marketing email:** consent and unsubscribe evidence.
- **AI:** provider/data disclosures, limitations, safety, and human oversight.
- **Sensitive or children's data:** specialist privacy/security/legal review.
- **Third-party trackers:** tracker inventory and jurisdiction-appropriate choices.

## Universal internal baseline

- Data inventory and retention/deletion schedule
- Vendor/subprocessor register
- Access review and key rotation
- Incident response and breach communication
- Monitoring/on-call runbook
- Backup, restore test, and disaster recovery
- Vulnerability and patch management
- Change/release records
- Business-continuity contacts
- Document owner, version, approval date, and next review date
